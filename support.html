MatchyLand support

Find quick answers or report a problem during the upcoming closed beta.

MatchyLand is not publicly downloadable yet. Closed-beta access will be provided through Google Play when testing opens.

Report a problem

Open a GitHub support issue. Please include your Android version, device model, MatchyLand version, what you expected, and what happened. Do not post private information, PINs, or Play Console credentials.

Common questions

Does MatchyLand need an account?

No. Profiles are optional and remain only on your device.

Does it need internet access?

No. MatchyLand is built to work offline and the app does not request Android's Internet permission.

How do I erase my data?

Delete individual local profiles in the app, or remove all app data by uninstalling MatchyLand or clearing its storage in Android Settings.

Where can I change accessibility or sensory options?

Use Accessibility & Sensory from the main menu or Settings. Sensory-friendly mode can also be enabled from the welcome screen.

Is MatchyLand available on Google Play?

Not publicly yet. A closed beta is coming soon.
