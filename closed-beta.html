Google Play closed beta

MatchyLand is in release preparation. Tester access will open after the Play Console listing and closed track are approved.

Candidate0.2.5Build 70
PlatformAndroidAndroid 8.0+

What testers can explore

  • Classic, Relax, Timed, Limited Moves, Progressive, Learning Pairs, Daily, and Custom modes
  • Kids and Adult experiences with 400 levels per difficulty
  • Eight educational matching activities and illustrated card collections
  • Thirty selectable in-app languages
  • Sensory-friendly settings, hints, local profiles, achievements, and statistics
  • Offline play with no ads, analytics, or account

How access will work

  1. Approved testers will receive the official Google Play opt-in link.
  2. Testers must join with the invited Google account.
  3. Installation and updates will be handled by Google Play.
  4. Feedback can be shared privately through the testing channel or the support page.
Safety note: no APK is offered on this website or repository. Install MatchyLand only from the official Google Play testing link when it becomes available.

Current status

The signed candidate has passed the project's automated unit, connected-device, lint, and release-build checks. Closed-beta dates and invitations have not yet been announced.
