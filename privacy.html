Privacy Policy

MatchyLand is designed to work privately and completely offline.

Plain-language summary: no account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no internet permission.

Information collection

MatchyLand does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. The app does not include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, telemetry, account, social, or remote-content SDKs.

Information stored on your device

Optional nicknames, preferences, parental-control PIN hashes, game progress, achievements, and game history are stored only in the app's private storage on your Android device. Guest play asks for no identifying information and guest progress is temporary.

Permissions and network access

The production app does not request the Internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts, notification, or device-identifier permissions. Vibration may be used when enabled in app settings.

Backups and deletion

Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup are disabled. You can delete an individual local profile in the app. Uninstalling MatchyLand or clearing its storage in Android Settings removes all local app data.

Children and families

MatchyLand does not ask for a birth date, email address, phone number, advertising identifier, precise location, or online account. It contains no advertising or external social features.

Changes to this policy

If MatchyLand's data practices change, this policy will be updated before the affected version is released.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, use the MatchyLand support page.
